About the Artist

Meet Paridhi

Paridhi is a handmade resin artist with a soft spot for the things we wish could last forever — the rose from a first date, petals from a wedding bouquet, a photograph that feels like home.

Working patiently in small batches, she dries, arranges and pours each piece by hand, sealing real flowers, petals, photos and keepsakes inside crystal-clear resin. No two pieces are ever exactly alike — that's the beauty of handmade.

Caring for your resin art

  • ✿Keep away from direct sunlight for long hours — it keeps colours bright and resin crystal-clear.
  • ✿Wipe gently with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals and alcohol sprays.
  • ✿Resin is sturdy but not unbreakable — treat frames, clocks and blocks like the keepsakes they are.
  • ✿Coasters love warm mugs; just avoid placing very hot pans directly on them.
