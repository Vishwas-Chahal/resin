Shipping Policy

  • We ship within India only — shipping is FREE on every order. All orders are prepaid via Razorpay; we do not offer cash on delivery. No hidden charges.
  • Every piece is handmade to order by Paridhi. After you place an order, she confirms the making timeline with you over email before your piece ships.
  • Orders are dispatched via trusted courier partners with protective packaging suited for resin art.
  • You can track your order anytime using your order ID (it looks like RBP-XXXXXX) on the Track Order section of the shop.

Refund & Return Policy

  • Because each piece is handmade and customised to order (names, photos, preserved flowers), orders are final sale once making has begun.
  • If your piece arrives damaged in transit or defective, tell us within 7 days of delivery at chahalravish777@gmail.com with your order ID and photos — you will get a free replacement or a full refund, your choice.
  • If an order cannot be fulfilled at all (for example, a material needed for your custom piece is unavailable), you receive a full refund with no questions.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7–10 business days. Payments are processed securely via Razorpay.

Terms of Service

  • All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and include the handmade piece itself; shipping is free on all prepaid orders.
  • We deliver within India only. Orders with addresses outside India cannot be accepted.
  • Product photos show real handmade pieces; because each one is poured by hand, small variations in swirls, colour flow and glitter placement are part of the craft — no two pieces are identical.
  • Custom Request pieces are confirmed over email (design, price and timeline) before payment is taken.

Privacy Policy

  • When you order, we collect only what we need to fulfil it: your name, delivery address, phone number and email.
  • Your details go directly to Paridhi by email and are used only for your order and delivery. They are never sold, rented or shared for marketing.
  • Payment details are handled entirely by Razorpay on their secure checkout — we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Want your details removed? Write to chahalravish777@gmail.com and we will delete them.
